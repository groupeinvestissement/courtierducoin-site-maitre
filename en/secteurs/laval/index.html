Frequently asked questions

Laval overview in Laval

Is a Laval-wide average enough to set a price?

No. Regional data provides context, but a review must consider relevant comparable sales, the micro-market and the property’s actual characteristics.

Which Laval areas do you serve?

Pierre can review a project anywhere in Laval, including Chomedey, Duvernay, Fabreville, Sainte-Rose, Sainte-Dorothée and Laval-des-Rapides.

Should I renovate before selling?

Not automatically. Prioritize work that improves presentation or reduces a likely objection, then compare its cost with its probable impact.

Can you help with a condo or plex?

Yes. The process is adapted to the property type, documents, occupancy and timeline.
