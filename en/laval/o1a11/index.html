No. Listing a property does not automatically suspend a process. Dates and obligations must be confirmed with the appropriate professionals.

Can I speak with Pierre without committing to sell?

Yes. A first conversation can clarify the situation and possible real estate options.

Is a direct offer always the best option?

Not necessarily. Price, conditions, timing, net proceeds and the real probability of closing should be compared.
