Frequently asked questions

Does a notice mean the lender already owns my property?

No. A notice is a preliminary step. The document and its dates still need serious attention.

Does the 60-day period begin when I receive the letter?

Not necessarily. For an immovable, the period generally runs from registration of the notice. Verify the exact date.

Does listing the property stop the process?

No. Marketing the property does not automatically suspend a mortgage or municipal process.

Can I sell before the deadline?

A sale may be possible if the value, amounts owing, timing and coordination make it workable.

Will my financial situation be advertised?

No. Personal and strategic information is not a marketing argument.

What if I do not know which notice I received?

Start with the exact title and date. Pierre can help organize the real estate questions and direct you to the appropriate source.
