Frequently asked questions

How is a duplex or triplex valued?

Comparable sales, income, expenses, condition and configuration must be reviewed together.

Do below-market rents affect a sale?

Actual income and leases are part of the buyer’s analysis.

Should a unit be renovated first?

Not automatically. Priorities depend on the building and market.

What numbers will buyers request?

Income, expenses, leases, occupancy, work and operating characteristics.

Can I plan a sale for next year?

Yes. Documents and work can be organized well in advance.
