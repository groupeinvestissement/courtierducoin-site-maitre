Frequently asked questions

Can preparation begin before every document is available?

Yes. Value, market context, condition and preparation priorities can often be reviewed first.

Who can sign for an estate?

It depends on the liquidator’s powers, the will, authorizations and the estate file. Legal confirmation may be required.

What is a declaration of transmission?

It records the transfer of rights related to the property and is generally prepared with a notary.

Must every heir agree to sell?

Required signatures depend on the file and granted powers. Do not assume every estate works the same way.

Should an inherited property be renovated?

Not automatically. Review market impact, costs and delays before undertaking work.

Can the file be coordinated from outside Montréal?

Yes, much of the real estate work can be coordinated remotely, subject to each document’s requirements.
