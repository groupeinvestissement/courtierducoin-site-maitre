Frequently asked questions

How do I know what my condo is worth?

Use comparable units with similar size, floor, light, features and building type.

Which documents should I gather?

Declaration, rules, minutes, financial statements, budget, reserve-fund information and work records.

Do the reserve fund and planned work affect the sale?

Yes. They influence buyer questions and understanding of the building.

Should I renovate the unit?

Not necessarily. Separate useful corrections from unnecessary spending.

Do condo fees affect buyer perception?

Yes. Review them with services, inclusions, management and the building’s condition.
