Frequently asked questions

How do I know what my house is worth?

Start with relevant comparable sales and the property’s specific characteristics.

Should I renovate before selling?

Not necessarily. Establish priorities before spending.

When should preparation begin?

Ideally several weeks or months before the target date.

Should I sell before buying?

It depends on finances, market conditions and risk tolerance.

Is the analysis automated?

No. It considers the property, comparables, competition and local context.
