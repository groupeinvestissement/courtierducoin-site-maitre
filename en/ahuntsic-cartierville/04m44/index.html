Frequently asked questions

House · Ahuntsic-Cartierville

Should I complete major renovations before selling?

Not automatically. Cost, timing and likely buyer impact should be assessed first.

Which documents should I prepare?

Certificate of location, tax bills, invoices, warranties, permits and available reports are useful starting points.

Are active listings comparables?

They show competition, but closed sales are needed to understand what buyers actually accepted.

Should I sell before buying?

It depends on financing, risk tolerance, the next property and timing.
