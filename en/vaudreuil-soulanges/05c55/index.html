Frequently asked questions

Condo · Vaudreuil-Soulanges

Which co-ownership documents help?

Declaration, rules, minutes, finances, insurance, reserve-fund study and maintenance log when available.

Do higher fees prevent a sale?

They should be explained with services, maintenance and reserves.

Is the regional condo median my value?

No. Comparable units and direct competition remain essential.

When should documents be requested?

As early as possible to avoid delays.
