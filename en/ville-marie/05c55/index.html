Frequently asked questions

Condo · Ville-Marie

Which documents should I prepare?

Declaration, rules, minutes, financial statements, budget, insurance and reserve-fund documents when available.

Do high fees prevent a sale?

They should be explained in the context of services, maintenance, reserves and building condition.

Is the median price my price?

No. It places the segment in context and does not replace comparable units.

When should I request syndicate documents?

As early as possible, because an incomplete file can slow a buyer’s review.
