Frequently asked questions

Condo · CDN / Côte-Saint-Luc

Which documents should I prepare?

Depending on the co-ownership: declaration, rules, minutes, financial statements, budget, insurance, reserve fund study and maintenance log when available.

Do high fees prevent a sale?

They should be explained in the context of services, maintenance, reserves and overall condition.

Is an area median my condo’s value?

No. It provides context but does not replace comparable units and direct competition.

When should documents be requested?

As early as possible, because an incomplete file can slow a buyer’s review.
